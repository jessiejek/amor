How It Works

Simple & Efficient Process

01

Schedule an Appointment

Reach out via phone, messenger, or email to set a convenient time. Walk-ins are also welcome during office hours.

02

Prepare Your Documents

Bring valid government-issued ID and the documents to be notarized. We will guide you on any additional requirements.

03

Appear in Person

Philippine law requires personal appearance of signatories before a notary public for identity verification.

04

Receive Your Notarized Document

Documents are stamped, signed, and recorded in the Notarial Register — legally valid and recognized nationwide.
